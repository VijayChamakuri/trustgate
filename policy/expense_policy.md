# Corporate Card & Expense Policy (FY26)

Every agent decision must cite the section(s) below. Section IDs are stable and machine-readable.

## 2. Meals

- **§2.1 Per-person caps.** Client entertainment is capped at **$75 per attendee**, team meals at **$30 per attendee**, including tax and tip. Over-cap meals go to review.
- **§2.2 Alcohol.** Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment. Alcohol on team or solo meals is not reimbursable.

## 3. Travel

- **§3.1 Airfare class.** Economy and premium economy are allowed. Business or first class requires VP approval and goes to review.
- **§3.2 Lodging.** Nightly room rate (pre-tax) is capped at **$350**, or **$450** in New York and San Francisco. Over-cap stays go to review.

## 4. Software

- **§4.1 New software vendors.** Software from a vendor that is not in the approved vendor registry must go through procurement. Card charges over **$500** from unregistered software vendors go to review.

## 5. Prohibited and personal spend

- **§5.1 Prohibited merchants.** Gambling, gift cards / stored value, cash withdrawals, personal streaming and gym memberships are never reimbursable.
- **§5.2 Personal purchases.** Personal purchases on the corporate card are not reimbursable and must be repaid.

## 6. Receipts

- **§6.1 Receipt required.** A receipt is required for any charge of **$75** or more (auto-billed subscriptions excepted).
- **§6.2 Receipt must match.** The receipt total must reconcile to the charge (meals may exceed the receipt subtotal by up to 25% for tax and tip).

## 7. Limits

- **§7.1 Split transactions.** Splitting one purchase into several charges to stay under a limit is prohibited.
- **§7.2 Single-transaction limit.** Non-travel charges over the cardholder's single-transaction limit require pre-approval and go to review.

## 8. Timing

- **§8.1 Weekend spend.** Non-travel, non-subscription spend on weekends goes to review.

## 9. Integrity

- **§9.1 Duplicate charges.** Charges with the same merchant, card and amount within 3 days are treated as potential duplicates and go to review for dispute.
